| Reconstructing Hyperspectral Images from RGB Images by Multi-Scale Spectral–Spatial Sequence Learning (2025) | Mamba / State-Space Models (SSMs) | Integrates the spectral dimension into the sequence scanning process (using "Continuous Spectral-Spatial Scan"). This forces the network to natively learn the complex inter-channel relationships between color bands . |
| Spectral Reconstruction from RGB Imagery: A Potential Option for Infinite Spectral Data? (2024) | MST++ and A++ Evaluative Models | Evaluates how close reconstructed bands actually are to the real ground truth (e.g., using the CAVE and SIDQ datasets) and analyzes whether "reconstructed color" mathematically matches physical reflectance . |
| RGB to Spectral Reconstruction via Learned Basis Functions and Weights (2020) | Unsupervised & Supervised Basis Mapping | Rather than forcing the neural network to map 3 channels directly to 31 channels, this paper trains a network to predict the weights of a few fundamental principal components (PCA), stabilizing the mapping process . |
| Band Reconstruction Using a Modified UNet for Sentinel-2 Images (2023) | UNetBRec CNN | Simulates a worst-case scenario where an entire spectral band (e.g., Near-Infrared) is completely missing. By inputting the remaining 11 functional Sentinel-2 bands, a modified U-Net predicts the missing band with extreme structural accuracy . |
| Deep Learning-Based Spectral Reconstruction Technology for Water Color Remote Sensing (2025) | DSR-Net (Residual CNN) | Targets Landsat and Sentinel-2 imagery to reconstruct 15 specific water-color bands from limited source bands. This enables highly sensitive aquatic environmental monitoring (like cyanobacteria mapping) using cheap satellite data . |
| Gap-Filling and Missing Information Recovery for Time Series of MODIS Data (2022) | Spatio-Temporal Deep Learning | Uses deep learning to fill gaps (like cloud-covered regions or broken sensor bands) by mapping both local spectral correlations and historical time-series trends . |
